`labels.csv` lists every crop with its grade and split: `crop_id, photo, label, split, subset, al_round, source, label_blind`. It's generated by `scripts/export_manifests.py`. Images are not stored here; `insarag_triage.data` downloads them from Roboflow and matches them by `crop_id`.

`split = excluded` marks crops left out of all final models. These are copies of a test crop (same picture), crops from a test-2 or test-3 photo, and two test-3 crops whose photo also appears in training under another name. 34 crop ids appear twice: once as an excluded active-learning row, and once in test 2 with the blind label. Images are matched by `source` and `crop_id` together.
